Predicting song chart longevity

Apr to May 2026

A three-person course project on weekly Spotify charts: 5.4 million rows, 110,000 songs, 77 countries. We asked whether where a song debuts predicts how long it stays on the charts.

Python, pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost, SHAP

Results

Geography only0.20
Plus artist history, song, genre0.33
Plus week-2/3 stream trajectory0.75

Cross-validated R² of XGBoost predicting log(1 + weeks on chart), nested 5-fold. Lasso scored lower each time (0.09 with geography alone).

Early stream growth dominated the SHAP importance ranking. That last result uses the first three weeks of data, so it answers a different question than the first two bars.
