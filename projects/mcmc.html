Starspot MCMC

Mar 2025

Group project for Astrophysics Laboratory (AST 51), Tufts.

A Metropolis MCMC sampler written from scratch in Python that can fit models with custom priors, applied first to simple test models and then to real starspot data from the eclipsing binary CM Draconis.

Open the full notebook Download the .ipynb

Python, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib

How it works

The notebook builds the sampler in stages: a two-parameter line fit and a four-parameter quadratic fit, both with normal priors, then a sine curve (amplitude and frequency) with flat priors fit to the CM Draconis spot modulation flux.

It explains how the likelihood, prior and posterior fit together, how the jump size affects the acceptance rate and burn-in, and how to read the trace plots.
